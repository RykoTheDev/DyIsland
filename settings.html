    Google

    Shows your Google Calendar events and Tasks (dots on the dates, a tooltip when you point at one) and the unread count of Gmail. Read-only: DyIsland can look, never change or send.

    Your Google client Google only lets an app use your account through a client you create: in the Google Cloud console make a free project, turn on the Calendar, Tasks and Gmail APIs, and create an OAuth client of type "Desktop app". Paste its ID and secret here. They stay on this PC and are left out when you export your settings.

    Not connected.

    Connecting opens Google's own sign-in page in your browser. The sign-in is stored encrypted by Windows. Disconnect revokes it at Google and deletes it from this PC.

    Coding agents

    Toasts, the working chip and (for Claude Code) permission prompts. Claude Code has its own setup above. For the others, copy the lines for your agent and paste them into its config file, then start a new session.

    DNS providers

    Pick the DNS providers you want one click away (a tile in the quick actions steps through them). Installing asks Windows for administrator approval once and shows you the list first. After that, switching needs no approval.

    Your own provider

    Checking...

    Only addresses you can see here can be switched to: the list is saved where only administrators can change it, so no other program can point your DNS somewhere else. Loopback and invalid addresses are refused.

    NextDNS switch

    Checking...

    Setup asks for administrator approval once. It does not change your DNS: it records your current NextDNS settings and lets only you switch between them and automatic DNS (your ISP). Off puts your connection back on automatic DNS.
